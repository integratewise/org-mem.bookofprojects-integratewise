# Solo Founder Guide - IntegrateWise Brand Hub

## Your Simple Workflow

**Problem**: Managing brand across 10+ tools is overwhelming when you're solo.
**Solution**: One place to update everything, then export what you need.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (Start Here)

**Go to: `/quick-start`**

This is your **command center** as a solo founder.

### What You Can Do Here:

#### 1. Edit Content (Left Side)
- **One-Liner** - Used on business cards, email signatures
- **Short Description** - Used on LinkedIn, website
- **Key Benefits** - Used in sales decks, landing pages  
- **Call to Action** - Used everywhere

**AI Help**: Click the ✨ Sparkle button next to any field to get AI suggestions.

#### 2. Export for Use (Right Side)

**Office Supplies (Print First)**:
- Business Cards → PDF for printer
- Letterhead → PDF for printer
- Company Seal → SVG for embossing stamp
- Email Signature → HTML for Gmail/Outlook

**Social Media (Promote)**:
- LinkedIn Banner → PNG
- LinkedIn Posts → 3 ready templates
- WhatsApp Catalog → Product templates

**Quick Copy**: Your tagline, website, email - one click to copy

---

## 🔄 Your Daily Workflow

### When You Need to Update Something:

```
1. Go to Quick Start
2. Edit the content you want to change
3. Click "Save All Changes"
4. Export what you need (business cards, posts, etc.)
5. Send to printer / post on social
```

### Content Flow:

```
Quick Start (you edit here)
    ↓
Automatically updates:
    - Stationery (letterhead, cards)
    - LinkedIn page content
    - WhatsApp templates
    - All brand assets
```

## 💾 Backup Your Work

The system automatically:
- Saves versions of all content changes
- Backs up everything to your browser storage
- Can export full backup as JSON

**To backup manually**:
1. Go to Control Panel
2. Click "Create Backup"
3. Download the file

## 🔒 Keeping Your Data Safe

All your data is stored:
- In your browser (localStorage)
- Never sent to external servers (except AI queries if you use the assistant)
- Exportable anytime as backup

**To move to a new computer**:
1. Export backup from old computer
2. Import backup on new computer
